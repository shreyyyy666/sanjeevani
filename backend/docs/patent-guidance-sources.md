# Patent Guidance Sources

The Patent Guidance area is educational and must not provide a legal opinion, filing deadline, or patentability determination. Its source cards should show the jurisdiction, source URL, and last-review date before users act.

| Source | Educational role | URL |
|---|---|---|
| WIPO Patents | General explanation that patent protection concerns inventions and that products and processes, including chemical compounds and production processes, may be relevant patent subject matter depending on applicable law. | https://www.wipo.int/en/web/patents |
| WIPO PCT System | Educational explanation of the international PCT pathway and the need to understand international and national-phase steps. | https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system |
| WIPO Lex / Indian Patent Office Manual | Historical procedural reference for the Indian patent system. The 2010 manual itself says it is a practical guide and not rulemaking; content must therefore be verified against current law, rules, and qualified professional review. | https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/7648 |
